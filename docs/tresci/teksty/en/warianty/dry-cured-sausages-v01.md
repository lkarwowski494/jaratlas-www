# Raw cold-smoked Polish sausage: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for raw Polish sausage smoked with cold smoke and eaten without cooking (Polish kiełbasa surowa wędzona). [T:-]

## !

- According to UGA, uncooked smoked sausages are smoked for flavor development and must be fully cooked before eating. [T:MIE-022]
- Cold-smoke temperatures are not hot enough to kill bacteria and may even help them grow. [T:MIE-076]

## Why

A raw sausage that is only cold-smoked has not been cooked. [T:-]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for a raw cold-smoked sausage that is eaten without cooking from a checked source. [T:-]
