# Beet and red wine preserves: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for beet preserves with red wine. [T:-]

## !

- Almost any food that is not very acidic, with a pH above 4.6, can support the growth of Clostridium botulinum and toxin production. [T:SLO-023]
- According to the FDA, the minimum temperature for growth and toxin production by Clostridium botulinum type E and nonproteolytic types B and F is 38°F (3.3°C). [T:MIE-059]

## Why

According to an FDA table of approximate values, beets have a pH of 4.9–5.6. [T:SLO-032]

Beet is a vegetable, and all fresh vegetables except most tomatoes are low-acid foods with a pH above 4.6. [T:ACI-005]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe that uses red wine to acidify beet preserves. [T:-]

This preserve is meant to be kept in the refrigerator. [T:-]
