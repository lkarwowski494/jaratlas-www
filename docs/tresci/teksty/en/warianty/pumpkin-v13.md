# Pumpkin ketchup: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for pumpkin ketchup, an acidified pumpkin purée processed in a boiling-water bath. [T:-]

## !

- NCHFP does not recommend home canning for pumpkin butter or any mashed or puréed pumpkin or winter squash. [T:SZY-015]
- NCHFP instructions for canning pumpkin and winter squash say: do not mash or purée. [T:ACI-036]

## Why

The whole product must contain a minimum, uniform level of acid so that botulism bacteria cannot grow. [T:ACI-017]

JarAtlas has found no tested home recipe for pumpkin ketchup from a checked source. [T:-]

JarAtlas has a card for frozen pumpkin. [T:-]
