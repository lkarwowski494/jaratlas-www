# Cucumber pickles in oil with garlic: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for canned cucumber pickles in a vinegar brine with oil and raw garlic. [T:-]

## !

- NCHFP research found that garlic-in-oil mixtures stored at room temperature are at risk of botulism. [T:OLE-001]
- There are no validated processes for vegetables and herbs in oil made at home; in the USA, the rules for such products apply only to commercial processors. [T:OLE-028]

## Why

The USDA Complete Guide has only 3 tested procedures for vegetables with oil: marinated peppers, marinated whole mushrooms and pickled three-bean salad, and all of them use acid. [T:OLE-036]

None of them is a cucumber pickle. [T:OLE-036]

Acidifying whole garlic cloves in vinegar is a lengthy and highly variable process, so there are no guidelines for acidifying whole garlic cloves at home. [T:ACI-437]

The whole product needs a minimum, uniform level of acid to prevent the growth of botulism bacteria. [T:ACI-017]

Do not change the proportions of vinegar, vegetables and water and do not use vinegar of unknown acidity; use only recipes with tested proportions. [T:ACI-016]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for cucumber pickles with oil and raw garlic from a checked source. [T:-]

JarAtlas has cards for tested cucumber pickle recipes without oil, such as fresh-pack dill pickles and bread and butter pickles. [T:-]
