# Mostarda di frutta: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for Italian mostarda di frutta, candied fruit in syrup flavored with mustard oil. [T:-]

## !

- Most mixtures of low-acid and acid foods have a pH above 4.6 unless the recipe contains enough lemon juice, citric acid or vinegar. [T:ACI-006]
- Almost any food that is not very acidic, with a pH above 4.6, can support the growth of Clostridium botulinum and toxin production. [T:SLO-023]

## Why

JarAtlas has found no checked source for the safety of mustard oil in mostarda. [T:-]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for mostarda di frutta from a checked source. [T:-]
