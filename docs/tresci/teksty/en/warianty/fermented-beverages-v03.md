# Jun: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for jun, a kombucha-like drink made with green tea and raw honey. [T:-]

## !

- According to the NCHFP, sugars other than the one in its kombucha recipe are not recommended: there are no data showing that alternative sugars keep the same level of safety. [T:FER-123]
- According to the BCCDC, raw honey can contain botulism spores, and if jun fermentation is incomplete or poorly controlled and the pH is not low enough, the spores can grow in the oxygen-free conditions. [T:FER-125]
- According to the BCCDC, too much gas in bottles and cans of kombucha or jun can burst the container under pressure. [T:NAP-014]
- Honey should not be fed to infants under 12 months of age: it is the one dietary source of Clostridium botulinum spores linked to infant botulism by both laboratory and epidemiologic studies. [T:SLO-036]

## Why

The BCCDC describes jun, fermented green tea with honey, in guidance for food businesses, not in a home recipe. [T:FER-124]

According to the BCCDC, jun generally contains more alcohol than kombucha. [T:FER-125]

JarAtlas has found no tested home recipe for jun from a checked source. [T:-]
