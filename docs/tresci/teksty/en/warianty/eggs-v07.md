# Cold-smoked quail eggs in garlic chili oil: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for cold-smoked quail eggs covered with olive oil, garlic and chili. [T:-]

## !

- There are no validated processes for vegetables and herbs in oil made at home; in the USA, the rules for such products apply only to commercial processors. [T:OLE-028]
- NCHFP research found that garlic-in-oil mixtures stored at room temperature are at risk of botulism. [T:OLE-001]
- It is unsafe to store home-prepared garlic or herb oils at room temperature. [T:MIE-095]
- Home-pickled eggs stored at room temperature have caused botulism. [T:MIE-083]

## Why

According to NCHFP, eggs are a low-acid food. [T:MIE-087]

JarAtlas has found no tested home recipe for eggs in oil from a checked source. [T:-]

JarAtlas has found no checked source showing that this keeps safely under oil, in or out of the refrigerator. [T:-]
