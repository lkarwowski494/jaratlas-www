# Stir-fry vegetable mix in brine: why there is no recipe here

## !

- According to Penn State Extension, broccoli, cauliflower, cabbage, kohlrabi and Brussels sprouts are not suitable for pressure canning. [T:SZY-025]

## Why

In the NCHFP mixed vegetables recipe, you may substitute other vegetables, except leafy greens, dried beans, cream-style corn, winter squash, sweet potatoes, broccoli, cauliflower or cabbage. [T:SZY-022]
