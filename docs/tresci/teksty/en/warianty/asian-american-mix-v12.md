# Stir-fry vegetable mix in brine: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for a stir-fry vegetable mix (such as Chinese cabbage, broccoli, bamboo shoots and bean sprouts) in brine, pressure-canned in jars. [T:-]

## !

- According to Penn State Extension, broccoli, cauliflower, cabbage, kohlrabi and Brussels sprouts are not suitable for pressure canning. [T:SZY-025]

## Why

The NCHFP recipe for mixed vegetables does not allow leafy greens, dried beans, cream-style corn, winter squash, sweet potatoes, broccoli, cauliflower or cabbage as substitutes. [T:SZY-022]

JarAtlas has found no tested home recipe for canning a stir-fry vegetable mix from a checked source. [T:-]
