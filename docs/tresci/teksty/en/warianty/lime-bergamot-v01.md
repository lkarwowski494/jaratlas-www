# Salt-preserved limes with dried chili (UCCE method)

This card uses the UCCE Master Food Preservers of Solano and Yolo Counties recipe for preserved lemons or limes, taken from the Ball Complete Book of Home Preserving, made with limes. [T:FER-105,FER-087]

## Ingredients

For this recipe: 10 limes and 1/2 cup pickling or canning salt. [T:FER-087]

Use at least 1 tablespoon of pure, non-iodized salt per fruit. [T:FER-089]

A UCCE Master Food Preserver handout lists bay leaf, oregano, cumin and various chili peppers among the spices for preserved Mexican limes. [T:FER-089]

JarAtlas rule (cautious): this card uses only dried whole chili peppers and bay leaves; fresh chili, garlic and onion from the same list are not covered. [T:FER-089]

JarAtlas rule (cautious): keep the amount of spices from the UCCE recipe, 4 bay leaves, and use up to 4 dried chili peppers in place of its 4 cinnamon sticks. [T:FER-105]

An Ask Extension expert answer calls the combination of lemons and salt very low risk for pathogens; the salt must be in an adequate amount. [T:FER-086,FER-104]

Do not reduce the salt. [T:FER-104]

## Preparing

Sterilize the jar by boiling it in water for 10 minutes, as the UCCE El Dorado recipe does. [T:FER-102]

Scrub the limes for packing in warm water and dry them. [T:FER-105]

Slice each from the top to within 1/2 inch of the bottom, almost into quarters, so that it stays attached at one end. [T:FER-105]

Juice the remaining 5 limes to measure 1 1/2 cups of juice. [T:FER-105]

Sprinkle 1 tablespoon of pickling salt over the bottom of the sterilized jar. [T:FER-105]

Pack at least 1 tablespoon of salt into each lime and place the limes in the jar with the dried chili and bay leaves. [T:FER-089]

Fill the jar with lime juice to within 1/2 inch of the top; the fruit must be submerged in juice. [T:FER-105,FER-104]

If it is not covered, juice an additional lime and add the juice to the jar; the UCCE handout gives this step for lemons. [T:FER-106]

Lemon and salt corrode a metal canning lid, so cover the jar loosely: with a loose plastic lid, or with a metal lid lined with plastic wrap and left loose. [T:FER-104]

## Ripening

The UCCE recipe ripens the jar at cool room temperature for 2–4 weeks, shaking it every day, or until the rinds are tender to the bite. [T:FER-105]

JarAtlas rule (cautious): Ask Extension says the fruit can be safely left at room temperature only for the first few days, to let the salt dissolve, and then suggests the refrigerator, so move the jar to the refrigerator after the first few days. [T:FER-104]

## Storing

Store the limes in the refrigerator. [T:FER-105]

JarAtlas rule (cautious): use them within 6 months, the limit the UCCE El Dorado recipe gives for preserved lemons. [T:FER-088]

Rinse a piece before use to remove excess salt. [T:FER-103]

## When to throw it away

Watch for sliminess or mold and throw the whole jar away if either appears. [T:FER-086]
