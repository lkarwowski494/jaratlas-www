# Home-canned sprats in oil: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for sprats canned at home in jars with oil. [T:-]

## !

- Pressure canning is the only recommended method for canning fish and seafood, and a boiling-water bath means a real risk of botulism for them. [T:SZY-006]
- According to NCHFP, ingredients that the original instructions do not call for should not be added to the jars, even with liquid, in research-based processes. [T:SZY-013]

## Why

The NCHFP pressure-canning process for fatty fish other than tuna, such as mackerel, salmon and trout, says: do not add liquids. [T:SZY-044]

Fish and seafood are low-acid foods, with a pH above 4.6. [T:SZY-001]

JarAtlas has found no tested home recipe for canning sprats in oil from a checked source. [T:-]
