# Boquerones en vinagre: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for Spanish boquerones en vinagre, raw anchovies cured in vinegar and kept in olive oil. [T:-]

## !

- According to the FDA, brining and pickling can reduce the parasite hazard in fish, but they do not eliminate it. [T:MIE-049]
- The EU requires food businesses that sell marinated, salted and other treated fish products to freeze the raw fish or the finished product if the treatment is not enough to kill live parasites. [T:MIE-053]

## Why

According to the FDA, freezing and storing fish at −20 °C (−4 °F) or below for 7 days in total kills parasites. [T:MIE-046]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for boquerones en vinagre from a checked source. [T:-]
