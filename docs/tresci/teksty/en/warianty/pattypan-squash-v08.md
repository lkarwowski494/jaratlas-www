## !

- Pressure canning is the only recommended method for canning meat, poultry, seafood and vegetables; using a boiling-water bath for these foods poses a real risk of botulism poisoning. [T:SZY-006]
- Canning summer squash without pickling is not recommended, because there is uncertainty about how to determine adequate processing times. [T:SZY-026]

## Why

Most mixtures of low-acid and acid foods are also low-acid, unless the recipe has enough lemon juice, citric acid or vinegar to make them acid foods. [T:SZY-002]

JarAtlas does not treat a dish as an acid food because it tastes sour, or because acid was added without a tested recipe. [T:-]
