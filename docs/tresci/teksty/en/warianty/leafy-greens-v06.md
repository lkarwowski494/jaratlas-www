# Spinach braised with garlic, preserved by tyndallization: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for spinach braised with garlic and fat, preserved in jars by tyndallization, that is, by heating the jars several times with pauses. [T:-]

## !

- Pressure canning is the only recommended method for canning meat, poultry, seafood and vegetables; using a boiling-water bath for these foods poses a real risk of botulism poisoning. [T:SZY-006]
- Tyndallization (repeated heating with pauses for spores to germinate) is not considered fully reliable, because some superdormant spores do not germinate between heatings. [T:SZY-009]
- According to NCHFP, fats such as butter or ghee should not be added to the jars, even with liquid, in research-based processes if the original instructions do not call for them. [T:SZY-013]

## Why

Botulinum spores are very hard to destroy at boiling-water temperatures. [T:SZY-004]

JarAtlas has found no tested home recipe for braised spinach with garlic in jars from a checked source. [T:-]

JarAtlas has a card for frozen spinach and other greens. [T:-]
