# Salted garlic paste: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for garlic paste preserved with salt. [T:-]

## !

- Salt and water activity barriers, other than drying, are not used as a home safeguard and are not recommended for consumers. [T:OLE-033]

## Why

The FDA list gives garlic a pH of about 5.80. [T:ACI-106]

Canning garlic is not recommended: it is a low-acid vegetable that needs a pressure canner, and adequate processing times have not been determined. [T:OLE-008]

Garlic-in-oil mixtures stored at room temperature are at risk of botulism. [T:OLE-001]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for salted garlic paste from a checked source. [T:-]
