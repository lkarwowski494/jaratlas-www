# Chinese salted eggs in brine: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for Chinese salted duck or hen eggs kept in brine (xian ya dan). [T:-]

## !

- According to the NCHFP, eggs are a low-acid food (pH 6.4–9.0); in pickled eggs, vinegar may not reach every part of the egg. [T:MIE-087]
- According to the NCHFP, never keep pickled eggs at room temperature except when serving, and then for no more than 2 hours in the 40–140 °F zone. [T:MIE-084]

## Why

JarAtlas has found no checked source with a method for salting eggs, so it applies the rule for pickled eggs here as a cautious analogy. [T:-]
