# Pork loin in aspic in jars: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for pork loin in aspic (meat jelly), pressure-canned in jars. [T:-]

## !

- NCHFP recommends canning low-acid foods, and tomatoes and tomato products, only according to the procedures in the USDA Complete Guide to Home Canning. [T:SZY-007]
- According to NCHFP, butter or ghee should not be added to jars, even with liquid, in research-based processes if the original instructions do not call for them. [T:SZY-013]
