# Fermented black beans with ginger: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for homemade douchi, Chinese fermented black soybeans, with ginger. [T:-]

## !

- Douchi caused a Bacillus cereus outbreak in China that affected 139 people; the strains were isolated from vomit and from the unprepared douchi, and two of them produced the emetic toxin cereulide. [T:NAP-066]
- Bacillus spores may germinate and produce toxin if cooked rice, barley or other cereals are not cooled within 2 hours before inoculation. [T:NAP-052]

## Why

JarAtlas rule (cautious): this guidance is written for cereals, and JarAtlas treats cooked soybeans with the same caution. [T:NAP-052]

According to the BC Centre for Disease Control, shiokara-natto (salted natto) is made from fermented black soybeans inoculated with koji (Aspergillus oryzae). [T:NAP-062]

That is a description of a process, not a tested home recipe for douchi. [T:-]

JarAtlas has found no tested home procedure for making douchi from a checked source. [T:-]
