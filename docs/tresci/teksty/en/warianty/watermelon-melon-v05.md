# Watermelon syrup (narsharab): why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for syrup made by boiling down watermelon juice (watermelon narsharab). [T:-]

## !

- SDSU Extension says NCHFP does not recommend canning watermelon; for watermelon rind pickles, SDSU points to a separate recipe. [T:ACI-066]
- Melons are low-acid foods with a pH above 4.6. [T:ACI-026]
- Almost any food that is not very acidic, with a pH above 4.6, can support the growth of Clostridium botulinum and toxin production. [T:SLO-023]

## Why

According to an FDA table of approximate values, watermelon has a pH of 5.2–5.8. [T:SLO-024]

The FDA list gives watermelon a pH of about 5.18–5.60. [T:ACI-156]

JarAtlas has found no checked source for the pH or water activity of watermelon juice boiled down to a syrup. [T:-]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for watermelon syrup from a checked source. [T:-]
