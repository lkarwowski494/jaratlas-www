# Teewurst: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for German teewurst, a raw smoked spreadable sausage that is eaten without cooking. [T:-]

## !

- According to UGA, uncooked smoked sausages are smoked for flavor development and must be fully cooked before eating. [T:MIE-022]
- Without an effective starter culture, fermentation temperatures of 80–100 °F can let dangerous bacteria grow in the meat. [T:MIE-025]
- Too much nitrate or nitrite can be toxic to people. [T:MIE-011]

## Why

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for teewurst from a checked source. [T:-]
