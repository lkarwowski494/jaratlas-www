# Porcini in olive oil: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for Italian-style porcini in olive oil with garlic (funghi porcini sott’olio). [T:-]

## !

- Drying and cooking do not destroy the toxins of poisonous mushrooms, and only an expert can tell edible species from poisonous ones. [T:SUS-035]

## Why

Botulism cases have been linked to mushrooms covered in oil: in the USA in 1998, to home-prepared mushrooms in oil, and in the United Kingdom, to two cases of mushrooms in oil. [T:OLE-026]

NCHFP research found that garlic-in-oil mixtures stored at room temperature are at risk of botulism. [T:OLE-001]

NCHFP says: do not can wild mushrooms. [T:OLE-040]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for wild mushrooms in oil from a checked source. [T:-]

## What you can do instead

NCHFP says that mushrooms can be frozen after they are steamed or heated in fat in a fry pan. [T:OLE-041]
