# Cured egg yolks: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for egg yolks cured in salt and sugar until firm enough to grate. [T:-]

## !

- The FDA advises using shell eggs treated to destroy Salmonella, such as pasteurized eggs, or pasteurized egg products in dishes with raw or undercooked eggs. [T:MIE-091]

## Why

Cured egg yolks are raw yolks that are not cooked. [T:-]

JarAtlas has found no tested home recipe for cured egg yolks from a checked source, whether made with pasteurized eggs or not. [T:-]
