# Swedish stirred lingonberries (rårörda lingon) for the refrigerator or freezer

This card follows the Marthaförbundet recipe for lingonberries crushed with sugar without cooking; JarAtlas keeps them within the NCHFP limits for uncooked jams: up to 3 weeks in the refrigerator or up to a year in a freezer. [T:ACI-756,SLO-020]

## !

- Uncooked fruit with sugar molds or ferments in a short time at room temperature, according to NCHFP, so keep the lingonberries only in the refrigerator or freezer. [T:SLO-020]

## Why lingonberries

A 2023 study of wild lingonberries measured a pH of 2.66–3.03. [T:ACI-752]

According to Marthaförbundet, lingonberries contain benzoic acid, which gives stirred lingonberries a long life in the refrigerator; the source gives no number of days. [T:ACI-756]

JarAtlas rule (cautious): because the source gives no number of days, JarAtlas uses the NCHFP limits for uncooked jams. [T:ACI-756,SLO-020]

## Ingredients

1 litre of lingonberries and 1 dl of sugar. [T:ACI-756]

## Preparing

Sterilize canning jars and prepare two-piece canning lids according to the manufacturer’s directions. [T:SLO-309]

Sort and wash fully ripe berries, drain them and remove any stems, as NCHFP directs for uncooked berry jam. [T:SLO-309]

Crush the berries with the sugar using a masher or a stick blender; a mixer gives a runnier result. [T:ACI-756]

Spoon them into the jars, close the jars and put them in the refrigerator or freezer straight away. [T:ACI-756,SLO-020]

## Storing

Store the lingonberries up to 3 weeks in the refrigerator or up to a year in a freezer. [T:SLO-020]

Keep an opened container in the refrigerator and use the lingonberries within a few days. [T:SLO-460]

JarAtlas rule (cautious): keep the jar on the middle shelf of the refrigerator, not in the door: Wisconsin Extension assumes 40 °F (4.4 °C) or colder for refrigerator pickles, while a Polish sanitary inspection office gives about 10 °C for the door, even though it suggests the door for jars of preserves. [T:ACI-523]
