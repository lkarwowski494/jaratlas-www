# Slippery jack and onion caviar: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for mushroom caviar from slippery jacks (maślaki) and onion, canned in jars. [T:-]

## !

- Drying and cooking do not destroy the toxins of poisonous mushrooms, and only an expert can tell edible species from poisonous ones. [T:SUS-035]

## Why

Wild mushrooms must not be canned. [T:SZY-020]

Do not attempt to can pureed vegetables, red meats or poultry at home: proper processing times for pureed foods have not been determined for home use. [T:SZY-014]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for canning wild mushrooms from a checked source. [T:-]
