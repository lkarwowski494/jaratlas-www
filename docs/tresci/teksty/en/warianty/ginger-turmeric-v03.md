# Ginger bug: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for a ginger bug, a fermented ginger starter used to make drinks (Polish zakwas imbirowy). [T:-]

## !

- According to Illinois Extension, its homemade ginger ale, a drink fermented in a closed bottle, is never made in glass: if the bottle stands too long without releasing carbon dioxide, it will explode. [T:FER-128]
- Health Canada names ethanol in non-alcoholic fermented drinks as a concern in kombucha, kefir and some other drinks, including ginger beer. [T:NAP-024]

## Why

Illinois Extension’s homemade ginger ale recipe uses active dry yeast, not a ginger bug. [T:FER-127]

JarAtlas has found no tested home recipe for a ginger bug from a checked source. [T:-]
