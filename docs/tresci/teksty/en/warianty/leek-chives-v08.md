# Chive oil for the refrigerator, eaten by the next day (UGA method)

This card uses the UGA Extension method for oil with non-acidified fresh herbs, here chives: the oil is heated to 180 °F (82 °C) before the herbs are added, and the oil is kept in the refrigerator or frozen. [T:OLE-018] UGA allows at most 4 days in the refrigerator, and NCHFP gives no more than 4 days for garlic in oil. [T:OLE-018,OLE-002] This card uses the shorter limit of the German Federal Institute for Risk Assessment (BfR): eat the oil no later than the day after you make it. [T:OLE-077]

## !

- Home-made oils with non-acidified garlic or herbs must never be stored at room temperature. [T:OLE-019]
- For garlic in oil, NCHFP says to make it fresh and keep it in the refrigerator at 40 °F (4.4 °C) or lower for no more than 4 days. [T:OLE-002]

## Why this oil needs care

Vegetables and herbs in oil meet the conditions for botulinum toxin production: spores can be present, oil keeps oxygen out, and the pH is above 4.6. [T:OLE-023]

You cannot tell from the look, smell or taste of the oil whether botulism is a risk. [T:OLE-027]

## What this card covers

It covers fresh chives added to heated oil; the acidification method was developed only for garlic, basil, oregano and rosemary and must not be used for chives. [T:OLE-016]

Using dried chives instead does not ensure a safe oil: without a laboratory measurement you cannot know whether dried herbs are dry enough. [T:OLE-020]

## Preparing

Make only as much oil as you will eat by the day after you make it. [T:OLE-077]

Wash the glass container thoroughly and boil it for 10 minutes. [T:OLE-058]

Choose good-quality chives, wash them thoroughly and pat them dry. [T:OLE-058]

Heat the oil to 180 °F (82 °C), checking it with a kitchen thermometer, and only then add the chives. [T:OLE-018]

Put the oil with the chives into the sterilized container, close it and keep it in the refrigerator. [T:OLE-018]

## Storing

Keep the oil in the refrigerator at 40 °F (4 °C) or lower. [T:OLE-057]

Eat the oil no later than the day after you made it, and throw away what is left. [T:OLE-077]

The BfR advises against making herb oils in advance and storing them at home. [T:OLE-076,OLE-087]

Alternatively, UGA allows freezing the oil until you are ready to use it. [T:OLE-018]
