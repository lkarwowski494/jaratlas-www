# Pumpkin and orange jam: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for pumpkin and orange jam. [T:-]

## !

- Pumpkin is naturally a low-acid food for canning, with a pH above 4.6. [T:SLO-045]
- Home canning is not recommended for mashed or puréed pumpkin or winter squash. [T:SLO-046]

## Why

According to an FDA table of approximate values, pumpkin has a pH of 4.8–5.2. [T:SLO-034]

Almost any food that is not very acidic, with a pH above 4.6, can support the growth of Clostridium botulinum and toxin production. [T:SLO-023]

The USDA has no tested recipes for safely canning certain pumpkin preserves and storing them at room temperature. [T:ACI-445]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for pumpkin jam from a checked source. [T:-]
