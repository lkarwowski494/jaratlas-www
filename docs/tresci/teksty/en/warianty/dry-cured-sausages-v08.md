# Metka: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for metka, a raw smoked spreadable sausage made from minced meat, with onion or in the salmon style, and eaten without cooking (Polish metka cebulowa and metka łososiowa). [T:-]

## !

- According to UGA, uncooked smoked sausages are smoked for flavor development and must be fully cooked before eating. [T:MIE-022]
- Cold-smoke temperatures are not hot enough to kill bacteria and may even help them grow. [T:MIE-076]
- Too much nitrate or nitrite can be toxic to people. [T:MIE-011]

## Why

Metka is eaten without cooking, so it does not meet this UGA rule. [T:MIE-022]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for metka from a checked source. [T:-]
