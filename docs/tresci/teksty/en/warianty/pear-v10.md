# Pear butter with chocolate: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for pear butter with chocolate. [T:-]

## !

- According to NCHFP, chocolate sauces are low-acid recipes and a risk for botulism, so recipes that can them in a boiling-water canner are especially risky; there are also no tested recipes for canning chocolate sauces in a pressure canner. [T:ACI-632]

## Why

According to Oregon State University Extension, fruit butters can be made from most high-acid fruits, among them pears. [T:ACI-627]

Oregon State University Extension gives a pear butter recipe made with pear pulp, sugar, grated orange rind, orange juice and nutmeg. [T:ACI-630]

Chocolate is a further addition outside any tested recipe. [T:-]

JarAtlas rule (cautious): the NCHFP warning is about chocolate sauces, not fruit butters, but JarAtlas treats chocolate or cocoa as a low-acid ingredient that is not part of any tested fruit butter. [T:ACI-632]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for pear butter with chocolate from a checked source. [T:-]
