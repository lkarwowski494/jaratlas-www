# Pumpkin butter: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for pumpkin butter. [T:-]

## !

- NCHFP and University of Georgia Extension have no recommendation and no process for canning pumpkin butter at home. [T:SLO-044]
- Pumpkin is naturally a low-acid food for canning, with a pH above 4.6. [T:SLO-045]
- Home canning is not recommended for mashed or puréed pumpkin or winter squash. [T:SLO-046]

## Why

According to an FDA table of approximate values, pumpkin has a pH of 4.8–5.2. [T:SLO-034]

NCHFP says that even when vinegar or lemon juice is added, the pH of pumpkin butter varies so much that no single recommendation can be made to ensure a safe product; a large amount of sugar may also not be enough to inhibit pathogens. [T:ACI-441]

Even if pumpkin butter were acidified to a pH below 4.6, its thick consistency would still need to be studied for how heat moves through the jar during processing. [T:ACI-443]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for pumpkin butter from a checked source. [T:-]

## What you can do instead

NCHFP says it is best to make pumpkin butter fresh for shorter storage in the refrigerator, or to freeze it. [T:SLO-044]
