# Cape gooseberry jam with vanilla: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for jam made from cape gooseberries (Physalis peruviana, also called goldenberries) with vanilla. [T:-]

## !

- According to Penn State Extension, like other nightshade plants, the unripe fruits of ground cherries (Physalis), as well as their stems and leaves, contain toxins; only ripe fruits are eaten. [T:SUS-301]

## Why

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for this jam from NCHFP, from Ball or from a pectin maker. [T:-]

JarAtlas has found no checked source for the pH of cape gooseberries, so it is not known whether the fruit is acidic enough for a boiling water canner. [T:-]

A boiling water canner is suitable only for acid foods with a pH of 4.6 or lower, and only with a tested recipe. [T:SZY-054]

Vanilla would be a further addition outside any tested recipe. [T:-]
