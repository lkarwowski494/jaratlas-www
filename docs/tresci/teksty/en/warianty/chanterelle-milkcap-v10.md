# Chanterelles in brine: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for chanterelles canned in brine. [T:-]

## !

- Drying and cooking do not destroy the toxins of poisonous mushrooms, and only an expert can tell edible species from poisonous ones. [T:SUS-035]

## Why

Wild mushrooms must not be canned. [T:SZY-020]

Low-acid foods must be processed in a pressure canner to be free of botulism risks; even so, the NCHFP page with the pressure-canning process for mushrooms says: do not can wild mushrooms. [T:FER-005,ACI-030]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for canning wild mushrooms from a checked source. [T:-]
