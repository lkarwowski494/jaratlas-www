# Moroccan harissa under oil: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for Moroccan harissa, a paste of chili and garlic kept under a layer of olive oil. [T:-]

## !

- There are no validated processes for vegetables and herbs in oil made at home; in the USA, the rules for such products apply only to commercial processors. [T:OLE-028]
- NCHFP research found that garlic-in-oil mixtures stored at room temperature are at risk of botulism. [T:OLE-001]

## Why

Peppers, garlic, mushrooms and eggplant have been involved in botulism outbreaks from vegetables and herbs in oil, mostly homemade products. [T:OLE-024]

JarAtlas has found no tested home recipe for harissa under oil from a checked source. [T:-]

JarAtlas has found no checked source showing that this keeps safely under oil, in or out of the refrigerator. [T:-]
