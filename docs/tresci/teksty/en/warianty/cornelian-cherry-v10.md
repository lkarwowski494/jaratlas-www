# Dried cornelian cherries for tea: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for drying cornelian cherries: JarAtlas has no recipe for them from a checked source. [T:-]

## !

- If the temperature is too low at the beginning of drying, microorganisms may survive and even grow before the food is adequately dried. [T:SUS-002]

## Why

Cornelian cherries are not in the drying tables JarAtlas uses, so there is no checked drying time for them. [T:-]

Home dehydrators are designed to dry food quickly at 140 °F (60 °C). [T:SUS-001]
