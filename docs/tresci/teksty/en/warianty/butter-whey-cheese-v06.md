# Porcini and truffle butter: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for butter with porcini mushrooms or truffles. [T:-]

## !

- Drying and cooking do not destroy the toxins of poisonous mushrooms, and only an expert can tell edible species from poisonous ones. [T:SUS-035]

## Why

There is no research-based information for consumers showing that adding herbs or vegetables to butter makes a product that is safe at room temperature or in the refrigerator. [T:OLE-034]

In 1993 the US FDA warned consumers not to prepare homemade spices in oil, margarine or butter for extended storage. [T:OLE-035]

NCHFP’s freezing advice covers butter alone and does not mention added mushrooms or truffles: freeze only high-quality butter made from pasteurized cream. [T:OLE-045]

JarAtlas has found no checked source on butter with mushrooms or truffles. [T:-]
