# Smoked sprats and herring: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for sprats and herring smoked at home, including whole, ungutted fish. [T:-]

## !

- According to the FDA, gut any fish before salting, drying, pickling or fermenting it. [T:MIE-061]
- The FDA considers uneviscerated (ungutted) fish that are salt-cured, dried or smoked a potentially life-threatening health hazard. [T:MIE-067]

## Why

According to the FDA, small uneviscerated fish under 5 inches, such as anchovies and sprats, may be left ungutted only if the process eliminates any toxin already formed and stops toxin forming during processing, and the finished product has 10% salt in the water phase (refrigerated) or a water activity below 0.85 or a pH of 4.6 or below (shelf stable). [T:MIE-066]

According to the FDA, such ungutted fish products are hazardous whether they are stored at room temperature, in the refrigerator or in the freezer. [T:MIE-070]

The FDA may act against uneviscerated salt-cured, dried or smoked fish more than 5 inches long. [T:MIE-069]

JarAtlas has found no tested home recipe for smoking ungutted sprats or herring from a checked source. [T:-]

## Gutted fish, hot smoked

Wisconsin Sea Grant and University of Minnesota Extension have a method for hot smoking fish at home that has been scientifically tested for safety. [T:MIE-072]

In that method, the fish are first gutted and gilled, the kidney is removed from the belly cavity, and all exposed surfaces are rinsed with fresh water. [T:MIE-143]

The internal temperature of the fish must reach 160 °F and stay there for at least 30 minutes; if your smoker cannot do this, finish cooking the fish in the oven within 2 hours of smoking. [T:MIE-071]
