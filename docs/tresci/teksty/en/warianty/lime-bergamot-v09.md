# Lime cordial for the refrigerator (Irish Times recipe)

This card follows the Irish Times lime cordial by Eunice Power, about 1 litre: lime juice, water, sugar and citric acid, simmered briefly, bottled and kept in the refrigerator. [T:ACI-758]

## !

- This is not a tested recipe and the source gives no number of days: University of Wisconsin Extension says untested recipes can be prepared and stored in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks, and not on the counter. [T:ACI-508,ACI-758]

## What this card covers

The US FDA list gives limes an approximate pH of 2.00–2.80. [T:ACI-145]

JarAtlas rule (cautious): the source does not measure the acidity of the finished cordial, so do not leave out the citric acid and do not add more water. [T:ACI-758,ACI-145]

## Ingredients

Juice of 10 limes, 1 litre of water, 500 g of sugar and 2 teaspoons of citric acid. [T:ACI-758]

## Preparing

JarAtlas rule (cautious): sterilize the bottles as UGA directs for flavored vinegars: wash them in warm, soapy water, then immerse them completely in boiling water for 10 minutes. [T:NAP-070]

Use non-corrodible metal or plastic screw caps, washed in hot soapy water, rinsed and scalded in boiling water. [T:NAP-071]

Bring the water and sugar to a boil. [T:ACI-758]

Add the lime juice and citric acid, stir well and simmer for 1–2 more minutes. [T:ACI-758]

Take the pan off the heat and let the cordial cool. [T:ACI-758]

Pour it into the sterilized bottles, close them and keep them in the refrigerator. [T:ACI-758]

## Storing

Use the cordial within 2 weeks and do not store it on the counter. [T:ACI-508]

JarAtlas rule (cautious): keep the bottles on the middle shelf of the refrigerator, not in the door: Wisconsin Extension assumes 40 °F (4.4 °C) or colder for refrigerator pickles, while a Polish sanitary inspection office gives about 10 °C for the door, even though it suggests the door for jars of preserves. [T:ACI-523]
