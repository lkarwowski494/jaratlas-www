# Fish garum with koji: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for fish garum, a fermented sauce made from mackerel or sardines with koji, in the style of ancient Roman garum. [T:-]

## !

- A study of botulism in Alaska found that toxin type E causes more than 85% of cases there, because many traditional foods, such as salmon heads, fish eggs, seal meat and whale blubber, are fermented in conditions that may favor Clostridium botulinum type E. [T:NAP-047] Botulism from toxin type E is linked only to eating aquatic animals. [T:NAP-047]
- The BC Centre for Disease Control reports that botulism outbreaks have been linked to ungutted fish used to make fesikh, a fermented salted fish. [T:NAP-048]

## Why

The Codex standard for fish sauce applies to fish sauce made by fermenting a mixture of fish and salt. [T:NAP-045]

The Codex standard also sets a limit for histamine in fish sauce. [T:NAP-046]

JarAtlas has found no tested home recipe for fish garum, with or without koji, from a checked source. [T:-]
