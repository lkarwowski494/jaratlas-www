# Candied pumpkin: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for candied pumpkin. [T:-]

## !

- Pumpkin is naturally a low-acid food for canning, with a pH above 4.6. [T:SLO-045]
- Almost any food that is not very acidic, with a pH above 4.6, can support the growth of Clostridium botulinum and toxin production. [T:SLO-023]

## Why

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for candied pumpkin from a checked source, and no tested process for pumpkin kept in syrup. [T:-]
