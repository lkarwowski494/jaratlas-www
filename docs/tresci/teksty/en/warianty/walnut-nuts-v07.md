# Homemade nut butter: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for homemade peanut, walnut, hazelnut or almond butter. [T:-]

## !

- In the NCHFP section on canning nuts and nut products, the only process is for green peanuts; NCHFP gives no canning process for peanut butter or other nut butters. [T:OLE-092]
- NCHFP says more recent research has shown that some species of Salmonella tolerate drier (low water activity) conditions. [T:OLE-091]

## Why

NCHFP no longer recommends canning a dry pack of shelled nutmeats at home, and there is currently no substitute canning process for dry packs of nutmeats. [T:OLE-090]

EFSA says aflatoxins can occur in foods such as groundnuts and tree nuts as a result of fungal contamination before and after harvest, and they are genotoxic and carcinogenic. [T:OLE-093]

JarAtlas has found no tested home recipe for nut butter from a checked source. [T:-]
