# Chicago-style giardiniera in oil: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for Chicago-style giardiniera, mixed vegetables kept in oil with chili and vinegar. [T:-]

## !

- It is hard to lower the pH of homemade vegetables in oil: vinegar mixes poorly with oil, so the vegetables themselves are not effectively acidified. [T:OLE-074]
- There are no validated processes for vegetables and herbs in oil made at home; in the USA, the rules for such products apply only to commercial processors. [T:OLE-028]
- Appearance, smell and taste cannot tell you whether vegetables in oil carry a botulism hazard. [T:OLE-027]

## Why

The BfR names peppers and chili among the vegetables in oil with which a household cannot make sure that C. botulinum will not grow and form toxin. [T:OLE-076]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for giardiniera in oil from a checked source. [T:-]
