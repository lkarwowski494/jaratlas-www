# Banana caramel jam: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for banana jam with caramel. [T:-]

## !

- NCHFP says its directions for canning fruit purées should not be used with bananas, dates, figs, Asian pears, tomatoes, cantaloupe and other melons, papaya, persimmons, ripe mango or coconut; there are no home canning recommendations for purées of these. [T:SLO-054]
- Almost any food that is not very acidic, with a pH above 4.6, can support the growth of Clostridium botulinum and toxin production. [T:SLO-023]

## Why

According to an FDA table of approximate values, bananas have a pH of 4.5–5.2. [T:SLO-027]

The FDA list gives yellow bananas a pH of about 5.00–5.29. [T:ACI-152]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for banana jam from a checked source. [T:-]
