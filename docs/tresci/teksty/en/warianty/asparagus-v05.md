# Asparagus in honey-mustard pickle: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for asparagus in a honey-mustard pickling liquid: JarAtlas has no recipe for it from a checked source. [T:-]

## !

- A boiling-water bath is suitable only for acid foods, such as fruits, pickles, jams and fruit butters, and only with the research-based process times of the USDA Complete Guide to Home Canning. [T:SZY-054]
- Do not change the proportions of vinegar, vegetables and water and do not use vinegar of unknown acidity; use only recipes with tested proportions. [T:ACI-016]
- Use only vinegar labelled 5% acidity; white and cider vinegar can be used interchangeably; do not use wine vinegars, homemade vinegar or lemon juice. [T:ACI-509]

## Why

Asparagus is a vegetable, and all fresh vegetables except most tomatoes are low-acid foods with a pH above 4.6. [T:ACI-005]

The FDA list gives asparagus a pH of about 6.00–6.70. [T:ACI-119]

The whole product must contain a minimum, uniform level of acid so that botulism bacteria cannot grow. [T:ACI-017]

Using a boiling-water bath for low-acid foods poses a real risk of botulism poisoning. [T:SZY-054]

Without a tested recipe, there is no checked process time or vinegar proportion for asparagus in honey-mustard pickle. [T:-]
