# Dried hawthorn and rowan berries: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for drying hawthorn and rowan berries. [T:-]

## !

- According to UW-Madison CIAS, raw rowan berries (European mountain ash, Sorbus aucuparia) contain parasorbic acid, described as a known carcinogen; heat treatment and, to a lesser extent, freezing may neutralize it, and the seeds probably contain hydrogen cyanide. [T:SUS-327]

## Why

The source says only that heat drying may neutralize parasorbic acid; it gives no drying time or temperature for it. [T:SUS-327]

Hawthorn and rowan are not in the drying tables JarAtlas uses, so this card covers both fruits together. [T:-]
