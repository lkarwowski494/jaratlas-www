# Ripe tomatoes pickled with onions: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for ripe tomatoes pickled in vinegar with onions. [T:-]

## !

- According to an FDA list, tomatoes have an approximate pH of 4.30–4.90, and the value varies widely with variety and conditions. [T:ACI-093]
- The FDA list gives white onions a pH of about 5.37–5.85. [T:ACI-107]
- Most mixtures of low-acid and acid foods have a pH above 4.6 unless the recipe contains enough lemon juice, citric acid or vinegar. [T:ACI-006]

## Why

NCHFP has a tested recipe for sweet pickled tomatoes with onions, but it is made with green tomatoes. [T:ACI-644]

Green tomatoes are more acidic than ripe ones. [T:ACI-050]

This card is about ripe tomatoes. [T:-]

Do not change the proportions of vinegar, vegetables and water and do not use vinegar of unknown acidity; use only recipes with tested proportions. [T:ACI-016]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for ripe tomatoes pickled with onions from a checked source. [T:-]
