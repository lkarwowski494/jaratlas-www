# Sichuan sweet-and-sour cabbage with chili: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for Sichuan sweet-and-sour cabbage with chili: JarAtlas has no recipe for it from a checked source. [T:-]

## !

- Untested recipes can be prepared and stored in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks; do not store them on the counter. [T:ACI-508]
- Use only vinegar labelled 5% acidity; white and cider vinegar can be used interchangeably; do not use wine vinegars, homemade vinegar or lemon juice. [T:ACI-509]
- Refrigerator pickles are not heat processed, so they must be kept in the refrigerator; do not decrease the vinegar in them, because an unsafe product may result. [T:ACI-501]

## Why

The NC State Extension and UC Master Food Preserver method for quick refrigerator pickles lists onions, cucumbers, asparagus, parsnips, radishes and pea pods as good vegetables to start with; it does not list cabbage. [T:ACI-505]

Cabbage is a vegetable, and all fresh vegetables except most tomatoes are low-acid foods with a pH above 4.6. [T:ACI-005]
