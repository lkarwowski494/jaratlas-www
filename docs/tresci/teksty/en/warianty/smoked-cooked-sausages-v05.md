# Thin pork kabanosy: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for thin pork kabanosy, smoked, cooked and dried sticks of sausage (Polish cienkie kabanosy wieprzowe). [T:-]

## !

- UGA recommends cooking products made from ground pork and beef to an internal temperature of 160 °F. [T:MIE-021]
- Too much nitrate or nitrite can be toxic to people. [T:MIE-011]
- According to UGA, a sausage is shelf stable only when its water activity is below 0.85 and its pH is under 5.3. [T:MIE-023]

## Why

UGA has a recipe for a smoked and cooked Polish sausage, but it is not a recipe for kabanosy. [T:MIE-031]

JarAtlas has no home method to measure water activity or pH. [T:-]

JarAtlas has found no tested home recipe for kabanosy from a checked source. [T:-]
