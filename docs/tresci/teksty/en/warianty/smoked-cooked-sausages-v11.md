# White sausage baked in jars: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for fresh white sausage with onion, baked in jars in the oven (Polish biała kiełbasa). [T:-]

## !

- Pressure canning is the only recommended method for canning meat, poultry, seafood and vegetables; using a boiling-water bath for these foods poses a real risk of botulism poisoning. [T:SZY-006]
- According to NCHFP, open-kettle canning and processing filled jars in an oven, a microwave oven or a dishwasher are not recommended, because these practices do not prevent all risks of spoilage. [T:SZY-010]
- According to UC ANR, oven canning is extremely hazardous. [T:SZY-011]

## Why

Red meats and poultry are low-acid foods. [T:SZY-001]

JarAtlas has found no tested home recipe for canning white sausage in jars from a checked source. [T:-]
