# Lecsó base: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for a lecsó base of peppers, onion and tomatoes, canned in jars. [T:-]

## !

- Pressure canning is the only recommended method for canning meat, poultry, seafood and vegetables; using a boiling-water bath for these foods poses a real risk of botulism poisoning. [T:SZY-006]
- In the tested NCHFP recipe for spaghetti sauce without meat, you must not increase the proportion of onions, peppers or mushrooms. [T:SZY-028]

## Why

Most mixtures of low-acid and acid foods are also low-acid, unless the recipe has enough lemon juice, citric acid or vinegar to make them acid foods. [T:SZY-002]

JarAtlas has found no tested home recipe for a canned lecsó base from a checked source. [T:-]
