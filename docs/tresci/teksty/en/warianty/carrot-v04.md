# Mexican carrot and jalapeño escabeche: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for Mexican-style escabeche: carrots pickled together with jalapeño peppers. [T:-]

## !

- Carrot is a vegetable, and all fresh vegetables except most tomatoes are low-acid foods with a pH above 4.6. [T:ACI-005]
- Do not change the proportions of vinegar, vegetables and water and do not use vinegar of unknown acidity; use only recipes with tested proportions. [T:ACI-016]
- Low-acid foods, with a pH above 4.6, such as beets, cannot be safely canned in a boiling-water bath unless they are acidified according to a tested recipe. [T:ACI-449]

## Why

The FDA list gives carrots a pH of about 5.88–6.40. [T:ACI-105]

The FDA list gives peppers a pH of about 4.65–5.45. [T:ACI-094]

NCHFP has a tested recipe for pickled carrots, but it contains carrots only, without peppers. [T:ACI-418]

Most mixtures of low-acid and acid foods have a pH above 4.6 unless the recipe contains enough lemon juice, citric acid or vinegar. [T:ACI-006]

The whole product needs a minimum, uniform level of acid to prevent the growth of botulism bacteria. [T:ACI-017]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for carrots pickled together with jalapeño peppers from a checked source. [T:-]
