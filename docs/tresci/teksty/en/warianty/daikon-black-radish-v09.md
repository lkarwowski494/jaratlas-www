# Black radish and honey syrup: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for a syrup made from raw black radish in honey, kept in the refrigerator. [T:-]

## !

- K-State says fermenting garlic in raw honey is risky: garlic is a low-acid food, honey varies from low to high acidity, and botulism has occurred in garlic in oil. [T:SLO-037]
- Honey should not be fed to infants under 12 months of age: it is the one dietary source of Clostridium botulinum spores linked to infant botulism by both laboratory and epidemiologic studies. [T:SLO-036]
- Almost any food that is not very acidic, with a pH above 4.6, can support the growth of Clostridium botulinum and toxin production. [T:SLO-023]

## Why

JarAtlas treats radish in honey with the same caution as garlic in honey. [T:-]

JarAtlas has found no tested home recipe for black radish and honey syrup from a checked source. [T:-]
