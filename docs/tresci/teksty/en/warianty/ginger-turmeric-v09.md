# Candied ginger: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for candied ginger, either kept in syrup (stem ginger) or dried. [T:-]

## !

- The FDA list gives ginger a pH of about 5.60–5.90. [T:ACI-125]
- Almost any food that is not very acidic, with a pH above 4.6, can support the growth of Clostridium botulinum and toxin production. [T:SLO-023]

## Why

According to Ask Extension, ginger and turmeric have pH levels above the safe level for canning in a boiling water bath. [T:SLO-482]

JarAtlas has found no tested process for ginger kept in syrup, and no checked source that gives a storage time for dried candied ginger. [T:-]
