# Beef tripe soup in jars: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for beef tripe soup (Polish flaczki), canned in jars. [T:-]

## !

- Pressure canning is the only recommended method for canning meat, poultry, seafood and vegetables; using a boiling-water bath for these foods poses a real risk of botulism poisoning. [T:SZY-006]
- NCHFP’s rule for home-canned soups is: do not add noodles or other pasta, rice, flour, cream, milk or other thickening agents. [T:SZY-017]

## Why

NCHFP directions for canning soups are meant only for ingredients that already have their own canning recommendations. [T:SZY-018]

JarAtlas has found no tested home recipe for canning beef tripe soup from a checked source. [T:-]
