# Sweet cherries in amaretto syrup: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for sweet cherries canned in syrup with amaretto (almond liqueur). [T:-]

## !

- Syrup helps keep the flavor, color and shape of the fruit, but it does not prevent spoilage. [T:ACI-067]
- The NCHFP recipe for whole cherries lists only water, apple juice, white grape juice or syrup as the liquid and does not mention vanilla or other additions. [T:SLO-201]

## Why

NCHFP has a tested recipe for canning whole sweet or sour cherries in a boiling-water canner. [T:SLO-095]

Amaretto is an addition outside this tested recipe. [T:-]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for cherries canned in syrup with amaretto from a checked source. [T:-]

JarAtlas has cards for sweet cherries canned by the tested NCHFP recipe, without alcohol. [T:-]
