# Garlic in chili brine, canned: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for garlic canned in a vinegar brine with chili. [T:-]

## !

- UC ANR says canning garlic is not recommended: garlic is a low-acid vegetable that needs a pressure canner, and adequate processing times have not been determined. [T:ACI-436]
- Acidifying whole garlic cloves in vinegar is a lengthy and highly variable process, so there are no guidelines for acidifying whole garlic cloves at home. [T:ACI-437]

## Why

The FDA list gives garlic a pH of about 5.80. [T:ACI-106]

Oregon State University Extension also says that pressure canning processing times have not been developed for plain garlic. [T:ACI-440]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for canning garlic in a chili brine. [T:-]

## What you can do instead

UC ANR describes covering peeled garlic cloves with undiluted vinegar and keeping them in the refrigerator, not canning them. [T:ACI-512,ACI-436]
