# Caramelized onion jam with wine: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for caramelized onion jam with wine. [T:-]

## !

- According to Ask Extension, onions are a low-acid vegetable, so they must be pressure canned to prevent botulism. [T:ACI-524]
- Almost any food that is not very acidic, with a pH above 4.6, can support the growth of Clostridium botulinum and toxin production. [T:SLO-023]
- According to the FDA, the minimum temperature for growth and toxin production by Clostridium botulinum type E and nonproteolytic types B and F is 38°F (3.3°C). [T:MIE-059]

## Why

The FDA list gives white onions a pH of about 5.37–5.85. [T:ACI-107]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe that uses wine to acidify onion jam. [T:-]

This jam is meant to be kept in the refrigerator. [T:-]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for caramelized onion jam from a checked source. [T:-]
