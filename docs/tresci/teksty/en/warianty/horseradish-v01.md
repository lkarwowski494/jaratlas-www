# Pickled horseradish sauce for the refrigerator (NCHFP)

This card describes the tested NCHFP pickled horseradish sauce: it is kept in the refrigerator and is not canned. [T:ACI-035,ACI-700]

## Ingredients

2 cups (3/4 lb) freshly grated horseradish, 1 cup white vinegar (5%), 1/2 teaspoon salt and 1/4 teaspoon ascorbic acid. [T:ACI-700]

Vinegar with less than 5% acidity should never be used in home food preservation. [T:ACI-516]

Do not change the proportions of vinegar, horseradish and the other ingredients. [T:ACI-016]

## Preparing

The pungency of fresh horseradish fades within 1–2 months even in the refrigerator, so make only small quantities at a time. [T:ACI-514]

Wash the horseradish roots thoroughly and peel off the brown outer skin. [T:ACI-712]

Grate the peeled roots in a food processor, or cut them into small cubes and put them through a food grinder. [T:ACI-712]

Combine the ingredients and fill them into sterile jars, leaving 1/4 inch of headspace. [T:ACI-700]

Seal the jars tightly and store them in the refrigerator. [T:ACI-035]

## Storing

According to NDSU Extension, the sauce keeps in the refrigerator for up to 3 months; it cannot be canned. [T:ACI-700]

JarAtlas rule (cautious): keep the jar on the middle shelf of the refrigerator, not in the door: Wisconsin Extension assumes 40 °F (4.4 °C) or colder for refrigerator pickles, while a Polish sanitary inspection office gives about 10 °C for the door, even though it suggests the door for jars of preserves. [T:ACI-523]
