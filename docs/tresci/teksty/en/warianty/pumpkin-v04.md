# Pumpkin pickled in cider vinegar: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for pumpkin pickled in apple cider vinegar. [T:-]

## !

- According to UGA Extension, pumpkin is a low-acid food, so it needs pressure processing for safe canning, just like vegetables and meats. [T:ACI-444]
- The USDA has no tested recipes for safely canning certain pumpkin preserves and storing them at room temperature; for cubed pumpkin, only pressure canning is recommended. [T:ACI-445]

## Why

The FDA list gives pumpkin a pH of about 4.90–5.50. [T:ACI-088]

A boiling water canner is suitable only for acid foods with a pH of 4.6 or lower, and only with a tested recipe. [T:SZY-054]

The whole product must contain a minimum, uniform level of acid so that botulism bacteria cannot grow. [T:ACI-017]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for pumpkin pickled in cider vinegar from a checked source. [T:-]
