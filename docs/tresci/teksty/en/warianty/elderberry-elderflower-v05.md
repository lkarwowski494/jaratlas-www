# Pontack sauce: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for pontack, a historical sauce of elderberries and vinegar that is aged before use. [T:-]

## !

- According to MU Extension, elderberries in a study had an average pH of 4.92 (range 4.73–5.19), while the maximum pH for safe canning of fruits and fruit products is 4.60; no research shows how much acid must be added to preserve elderberry products safely, and Extension recommends freezing the berries or juice instead. [T:SLO-480]
- Raw elderberries may contain elevated levels of toxic compounds, so always cook elderberries before eating them. [T:SLO-061]
- Elder stems and leaves contain higher levels of toxins, so remove them before processing. [T:SLO-062]

## Why

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for an elderberry vinegar sauce and no checked source for how long such a sauce can be kept. [T:-]

Aging the sauce means keeping it for a long time without a tested recipe. [T:-]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for pontack sauce from a checked source. [T:-]
