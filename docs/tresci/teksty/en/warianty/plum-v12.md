# Plum halves in syrup with rum: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for plum halves canned in syrup with rum. [T:-]

## !

- Syrup helps keep the flavor, color and shape of the fruit, but it does not prevent spoilage. [T:ACI-067]

## Why

NCHFP has a tested recipe for canning halved or whole plums in a boiling-water canner. [T:SLO-100]

Plums may be packed in water, or in very light, light or medium syrup. [T:ACI-186]

Rum is an addition outside this tested recipe. [T:-]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for plums canned in syrup with rum from a checked source. [T:-]

JarAtlas has a card for plums canned by the tested NCHFP recipe, without alcohol. [T:-]
