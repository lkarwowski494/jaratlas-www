# Garlic and herb butter: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for garlic and herb butter made to keep. [T:-]

## !

- NCHFP research found that garlic-in-oil mixtures stored at room temperature are at risk of botulism. [T:OLE-001]
- There is no research showing that adding herbs or vegetables to butter makes a product that is safe at room temperature or in the refrigerator. [T:OLE-034]
- In a 1993 bulletin, the FDA warned against preparing seasonings in oil, margarine or butter at home for extended storage. [T:OLE-035]

## Why

JarAtlas has found no tested home recipe for garlic and herb butter that keeps from a checked source. [T:-]
