# Uncooked strawberry jam for the refrigerator or freezer (NCHFP)

This card describes the NCHFP recipe for uncooked strawberry jam with powdered pectin: the fruit is not cooked and the jam is not canned; it keeps up to 3 weeks in the refrigerator or up to a year in a freezer. [T:SLO-490,SLO-020]

## Ingredients

For about 5–6 half-pint jars: 2 cups crushed strawberries (about 1 quart, about 0.95 l, of berries), 4 cups sugar, 1 package powdered pectin and 1 cup water. [T:SLO-490]

## Preparing

Sterilize canning jars and prepare two-piece canning lids according to the manufacturer’s directions. [T:SLO-309]

Sort and wash fully ripe strawberries, drain them, remove caps and stems, then crush the berries. [T:SLO-309]

## Making the jam

Mix the crushed berries well with the sugar and let stand for 20 minutes, stirring occasionally. [T:SLO-490]

Dissolve the pectin in the water and boil for 1 minute. [T:SLO-490]

Add the pectin solution to the berry-and-sugar mixture and stir for 2 minutes. [T:SLO-490]

Pour the jam into freezer containers or canning jars, leaving 1/2 inch (about 1.3 cm) headspace. [T:SLO-490]

Close the containers and let them stand at room temperature for 24 hours. [T:SLO-490]

## Storing

Then store the jam up to 3 weeks in the refrigerator or up to a year in a freezer. [T:SLO-020]

Do not keep uncooked jam at room temperature: it will mold or ferment in a short time. [T:SLO-020]

Keep an opened container in the refrigerator and use the jam within a few days. [T:SLO-460]
