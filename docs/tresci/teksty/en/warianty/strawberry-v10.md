# Strawberry kompot: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for Polish strawberry kompot: whole strawberries canned in a light syrup. [T:-]

## !

- NCHFP directions for canning whole berries are only for blackberries, blueberries, currants, dewberries, gooseberries, huckleberries, loganberries, mulberries and raspberries; strawberries are not on the list. [T:ACI-068]
- According to South Dakota State University Extension, NCHFP does not recommend canning plain strawberries; strawberries are commonly canned as jellies or jams. [T:ACI-604]

## Why

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for strawberry kompot from a checked source. [T:-]

## What you can do instead

NCHFP has tested recipes for strawberry jam with pectin and for strawberry preserves. [T:SLO-072,SLO-085]

JarAtlas has cards for strawberry preserves and frozen strawberries. [T:-]
