# Green wild garlic oil for the refrigerator, used within days (UGA method)

This card uses the UGA Extension method for oil with non-acidified fresh herbs, here wild garlic leaves: the oil is heated to 180 °F (82 °C) before the leaves are added, and the oil is kept in the refrigerator and thrown away 4 days after it was made, or frozen. [T:OLE-018]

## !

- According to BfR, young wild garlic leaves resemble those of the poisonous lily of the valley and the very poisonous autumn crocus; when in doubt, it is better not to eat them. [T:SUS-337]
- Home-made oils with non-acidified garlic or herbs must never be stored at room temperature. [T:OLE-019]
- Keep this oil in the refrigerator and throw it away 4 days after you made it, or freeze it. [T:OLE-018]
- NCHFP gives the same limit for garlic in oil: make it fresh and keep it in the refrigerator at 40 °F (4.4 °C) or lower for no more than 4 days. [T:OLE-002]

## Why this oil needs care

Vegetables and herbs in oil meet the conditions for botulinum toxin production: spores can be present, oil keeps oxygen out, and the pH is above 4.6. [T:OLE-023]

You cannot tell from the look, smell or taste of the oil whether botulism is a risk. [T:OLE-027]

## What this card covers

It covers fresh wild garlic leaves added to heated oil; the acidification method was developed only for garlic, basil, oregano and rosemary and must not be used for other herbs, so it is not used here. [T:OLE-016]

Using dried leaves instead does not ensure a safe oil: without a laboratory measurement you cannot know whether dried herbs are dry enough. [T:OLE-020]

## Picking the leaves

According to AGES, wild garlic is also confused with spotted arum, Solomon’s seal and wild tulips. [T:SUS-337]

JarAtlas rule: if you are not sure that every leaf is wild garlic, do not use the leaves. [T:SUS-337]

## Preparing

Make only as much oil as you will use within 4 days. [T:OLE-018]

Wash the glass container thoroughly and boil it for 10 minutes. [T:OLE-058]

Choose good-quality leaves, wash them thoroughly and pat them dry. [T:OLE-058]

AGES advises washing wild garlic leaves one by one, thoroughly, under hot running water before eating or freezing them. [T:SUS-338]

Heat the oil to 180 °F (82 °C), checking it with a kitchen thermometer, and only then add the leaves. [T:OLE-018]

Put the oil with the leaves into the sterilized container, close it and keep it in the refrigerator. [T:OLE-018]

## Storing

Throw the oil away 4 days after you made it. [T:OLE-018]

Alternatively, freeze the oil until you are ready to use it. [T:OLE-018]
