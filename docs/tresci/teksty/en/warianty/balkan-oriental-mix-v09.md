# Bulgarian kyopolou: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for Bulgarian kyopolou, a spread of roasted eggplant and peppers with oil. [T:-]

## !

- There are no validated processes for vegetables and herbs in oil made at home; in the USA, the rules for such products apply only to commercial processors. [T:OLE-028]
- Appearance, smell and taste cannot tell you whether vegetables in oil carry a botulism hazard. [T:OLE-027]
- The acidification method for homemade garlic and herb oils was developed only for garlic, basil, oregano and rosemary; it does not cover other vegetables or herbs, such as peppers or mushrooms. [T:OLE-016]
- According to ANSES, the French food safety agency, the foods that most often cause botulism include canned vegetables, such as peppers, asparagus, Greek-style olives and tapenade, and canned meat, such as terrine and pâté, usually homemade or artisanal. [T:OLE-046]

## Why

Peppers, garlic, mushrooms and eggplant have been involved in botulism outbreaks from vegetables and herbs in oil, mostly homemade products. [T:OLE-024]

JarAtlas has found no tested home recipe for kyopolou from a checked source. [T:-]
