# British piccalilli with mustard: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for British-style piccalilli: cauliflower and other vegetables in a mustard sauce thickened with flour or starch. [T:-]

## !

- Cauliflower is a vegetable, and all fresh vegetables except most tomatoes are low-acid foods with a pH above 4.6. [T:ACI-005]
- Most mixtures of low-acid and acid foods have a pH above 4.6 unless the recipe contains enough lemon juice, citric acid or vinegar. [T:ACI-006]
- In its Pickled Corn Relish, NCHFP allows the relish to be thickened with a flour paste if desired; JarAtlas treats this as an exception within that recipe and does not carry it over to other preserves. [T:ACI-423]

## Why

NCHFP has a tested recipe for pickled mixed vegetables with cauliflower, white vinegar of 5% acidity and prepared mustard; its ingredient list has no flour or starch. [T:ACI-636]

The whole product needs a minimum, uniform level of acid to prevent the growth of botulism bacteria. [T:ACI-017]

Do not change the proportions of vinegar, vegetables and water and do not use vinegar of unknown acidity; use only recipes with tested proportions. [T:ACI-016]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for British piccalilli thickened with flour or starch from a checked source. [T:-]
