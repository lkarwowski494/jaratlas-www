# Canned mushroom sauce: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for wild mushroom sauce canned in jars. [T:-]

## !

- Drying and cooking do not destroy the toxins of poisonous mushrooms, and only an expert can tell edible species from poisonous ones. [T:SUS-035]

## Why

Wild mushrooms must not be canned. [T:SZY-020]

NCHFP’s rule for home-canned soups is: do not add noodles or other pasta, rice, flour, cream, milk or other thickening agents. [T:SZY-017]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for canning wild mushrooms from a checked source. [T:-]
