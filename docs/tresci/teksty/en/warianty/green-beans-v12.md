# Canned green beans braised with garlic: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for green beans braised with garlic and fat, canned in jars. [T:-]

## !

- Pressure canning is the only recommended method for canning meat, poultry, seafood and vegetables; using a boiling-water bath for these foods poses a real risk of botulism poisoning. [T:SZY-006]
- According to NCHFP, butter or ghee should not be added to jars, even with liquid, in research-based processes if the original instructions do not call for them. [T:SZY-013]

## Why

All fresh vegetables except most tomatoes are low-acid foods. [T:SZY-001]

JarAtlas has found no tested home recipe for canned braised green beans with garlic from a checked source. [T:-]

JarAtlas treats oil or other fat added to jars with the same caution. [T:-]

JarAtlas has a card for green beans in water canned in a pressure canner. [T:-]
