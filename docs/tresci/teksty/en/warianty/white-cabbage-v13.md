# Canned cabbage with mushrooms: why not to can it

This card has no recipe, because the sources give no safe way to can cabbage with mushrooms. [T:SZY-020,SZY-025]

## !

- Drying and cooking do not destroy the toxins of poisonous mushrooms, and only an expert can tell edible species from poisonous ones. [T:SUS-035]
- Wild mushrooms must not be canned. [T:SZY-020]

## Why

Pressure canning is the only recommended method for canning vegetables, and a boiling-water bath means a real risk of botulism for them. [T:SZY-006]

According to Penn State Extension, cabbage is not suitable for pressure canning. [T:SZY-025]

Most mixtures of low-acid and acid foods are above the safe pH limit unless the recipe contains enough lemon juice, citric acid or vinegar. [T:SZY-002]
