# Candied rowan berries: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for candied rowan berries. [T:-]

## !

- According to UW-Madison CIAS, raw rowan berries (European mountain ash, Sorbus aucuparia) contain parasorbic acid, described as a known carcinogen; heat treatment and, to a lesser extent, freezing may neutralize it, and the seeds probably contain hydrogen cyanide. [T:SUS-327]

## Why

The source says only that heat treatment may neutralize parasorbic acid; it gives no time or temperature for it. [T:SUS-327]

JarAtlas has found no tested home recipe for candied rowan berries. [T:-]
