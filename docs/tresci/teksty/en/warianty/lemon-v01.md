# Preserved lemons in salt and lemon juice (UCCE, Ball recipe)

This card follows the UCCE Master Food Preservers of El Dorado County recipe for traditional preserved lemons, taken from the Ball Complete Book of Home Preserving: lemons in salt and their own juice. [T:FER-102]

## Ingredients

For 1 quart jar (about 946 ml): 10 lemons and 1/2 cup pickling or canning salt. [T:FER-102,ACI-174]

Spices are optional and many preserved lemon recipes use none, so this version uses only salt and lemon juice. [T:FER-102]

Choose top-quality lemons with no blemishes. [T:FER-103]

Ask Extension (Oregon State University) calls the combination of lemons and salt very low risk for pathogens; the salt must be in an adequate amount. [T:FER-086,FER-104]

Do not reduce the salt. [T:FER-104]

## Preparing

Sterilize the jar by boiling it in water for 10 minutes and keep it hot until you use it. [T:FER-102]

Wash 5 lemons in warm water, scrubbing them well, and dry them. [T:FER-102]

Cut each of them into quarters without cutting through the bottom end. [T:FER-102]

Juice the remaining 5 lemons to measure 1 1/2 cups of juice. [T:FER-102]

Sprinkle 1 tablespoon of salt over the bottom of the jar, pack 1 heaping tablespoon of salt into each lemon, place the lemons in the jar and cover them with the remaining salt. [T:FER-102]

Fill the jar with lemon juice to within 1/2 inch of the top; the lemons must be submerged in juice. [T:FER-102,FER-104]

Lemon and salt corrode a metal canning lid, so use a plastic lid or line a metal lid with plastic wrap. [T:FER-104]

## Ripening

The El Dorado recipe keeps the jar in a dark, cool cupboard for 2 weeks, shaking it every day. [T:FER-103]

JarAtlas rule (cautious): Ask Extension says the fruit can be safely left at room temperature only for the first few days, to let the salt dissolve, and then suggests the refrigerator, so move the jar to the refrigerator after the first few days. [T:FER-104]

The lemons are ready when the rinds are tender to the bite. [T:FER-105]

## Storing

To use, remove the pulp and membrane, keep only the peel, rinse it under water to remove excess salt and dry it. [T:FER-103]

Keep the lemons in the refrigerator in a container with an airtight lid for up to 6 months. [T:FER-088]

## When to throw it away

Watch for sliminess or mold and throw the whole jar away if either appears. [T:FER-086]
