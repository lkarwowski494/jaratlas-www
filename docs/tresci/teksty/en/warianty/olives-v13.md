# Olive and walnut paste: why there is no recipe here

## !

- There are no validated processes for vegetables and herbs in oil made at home; in the USA, the rules for such products apply only to commercial processors. [T:OLE-028]
- According to ANSES, the French food safety agency, the foods that most often cause botulism include canned vegetables, such as peppers, asparagus, Greek-style olives and tapenade, and canned meat, such as terrine and pâté, usually homemade or artisanal. [T:OLE-046]
- In France in September 2011, two family botulism outbreaks (type A toxin) were caused by a ground green olive paste from an artisanal producer that used incorrect sterilization techniques. [T:OLE-049]
