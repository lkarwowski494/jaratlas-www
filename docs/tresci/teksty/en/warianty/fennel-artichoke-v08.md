# Fennel and white wine preserves: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for fennel preserves with white wine. [T:-]

## !

- Fennel is a vegetable, and all fresh vegetables except most tomatoes are low-acid foods with a pH above 4.6. [T:ACI-005]
- Almost any food that is not very acidic, with a pH above 4.6, can support the growth of Clostridium botulinum and toxin production. [T:SLO-023]

## Why

JarAtlas has found no checked source for the pH of fennel. [T:-]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe that uses wine to acidify fennel preserves. [T:-]
