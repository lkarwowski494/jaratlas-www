# Cantaloupe jam with ginger: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for cantaloupe melon jam with ginger. [T:-]

## !

- Melons are low-acid foods with a pH above 4.6. [T:ACI-026]
- Almost any food that is not very acidic, with a pH above 4.6, can support the growth of Clostridium botulinum and toxin production. [T:SLO-023]

## Why

According to an FDA table of approximate values, cantaloupe melon has a pH of 6.17–7.13. [T:SLO-025]

The FDA list gives cantaloupe a pH of about 6.13–6.58. [T:ACI-157]

The FDA list gives ginger a pH of about 5.60–5.90. [T:ACI-125]

The University of California does not recommend canning low-acid fruits such as melon, because a boiling water bath is not hot enough to reduce the risk of botulism. [T:ACI-705]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for cantaloupe jam from a checked source. [T:-]
