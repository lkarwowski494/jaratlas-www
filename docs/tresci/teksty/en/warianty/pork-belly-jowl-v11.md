# Pure white lard in jars: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for pure white lard rendered for frying and confit and kept in jars: JarAtlas has no recipe for it from a checked source. [T:-]

## !

- In an Ask Extension answer about rendered chicken fat: at room temperature the fat goes rancid more easily, and if bits of chicken are left in it, botulism is a concern. [T:OLE-085]
- Extension and USDA have no canning recommendations at all for tallow or lard. [T:OLE-084]

## Why

Without a checked source, JarAtlas cannot say how long homemade lard keeps in jars or where to store it. [T:-]
