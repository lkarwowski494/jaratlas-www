# Smooth Dijon mustard with white wine and verjus: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for smooth French Dijon-style mustard made with white wine and verjus (juice of unripe grapes). [T:-]

## !

- Do not change the proportions of vinegar, food and water in a recipe and do not use vinegar of unknown acidity; use only recipes with tested proportions. [T:ACI-016]
- For quick pickles, Wisconsin Extension says: use only vinegar labelled 5% acidity; white and cider vinegar can be used interchangeably; do not use wine vinegars, homemade vinegar or lemon juice. [T:ACI-509]

## Why

In this mustard the acid comes from wine and verjus, and JarAtlas has found no checked source for their acidity. [T:-]

The FDA list gives mustard a wide pH range of about 3.55–6.00. [T:ACI-162]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for Dijon mustard made with wine and verjus from a checked source. [T:-]
