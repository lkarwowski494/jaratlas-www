# Greek-style fish in jars: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for Polish Greek-style fish (ryba po grecku), fried fish in a vegetable and tomato sauce, canned in jars. [T:-]

## !

- Pressure canning is the only recommended method for canning meat, poultry, seafood and vegetables; using a boiling-water bath for these foods poses a real risk of botulism poisoning. [T:SZY-006]
- NCHFP recommends canning low-acid foods, and tomatoes and tomato products, only according to the procedures in the USDA Complete Guide to Home Canning. [T:SZY-007]

## Why

Most mixtures of low-acid and acid foods are also low-acid, unless their recipes include enough lemon juice, citric acid or vinegar to make them acid foods. [T:SZY-002]

JarAtlas has found no tested home recipe for canning fish in a vegetable and tomato sauce from a checked source. [T:-]
