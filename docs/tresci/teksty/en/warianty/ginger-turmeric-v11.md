# Spiced ginger syrup: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for spiced ginger syrup. [T:-]

## !

- According to Ask Extension, ginger and turmeric have pH levels above the safe level for canning in a boiling water bath, and honey and sugar syrups can harbor botulism spores. [T:SLO-482]
- The FDA list gives ginger a pH of about 5.60–5.90. [T:ACI-125]

## Why

According to Ask Extension, there are no research-based recipes for canning infused simple syrups. [T:SLO-481]

Almost any food that is not very acidic, with a pH above 4.6, can support the growth of Clostridium botulinum and toxin production. [T:SLO-023]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for ginger syrup from a checked source. [T:-]
