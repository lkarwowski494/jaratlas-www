# Polish beans with sausage preserved by tyndallization: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for Polish beans in tomato sauce with a lot of sausage (fasolka po bretońsku), preserved in jars by tyndallization, that is, by heating the jars several times with pauses. [T:-]

## !

- Pressure canning is the only recommended method for canning meat, poultry, seafood and vegetables; using a boiling-water bath for these foods poses a real risk of botulism poisoning. [T:SZY-006]
- Tyndallization (repeated heating with pauses for spores to germinate) is not considered fully reliable, because some superdormant spores do not germinate between heatings. [T:SZY-009]
- NCHFP says not to add noodles or other pasta, rice, flour, cream, milk or other thickening agents to home-canned soups. [T:SZY-017]

## Why

Botulinum spores are very hard to destroy at boiling-water temperatures. [T:SZY-004]

JarAtlas has found no tested home recipe for Polish beans with sausage in jars from a checked source. [T:-]

JarAtlas has a card for dried beans in tomato sauce canned in a pressure canner. [T:-]
