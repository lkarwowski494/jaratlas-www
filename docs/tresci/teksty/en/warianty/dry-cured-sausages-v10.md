# ’Nduja: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for ’nduja, a spicy spreadable Calabrian sausage that is fermented, smoked and eaten without cooking. [T:-]

## !

- According to UGA, uncooked smoked sausages are smoked for flavor development and must be fully cooked before eating. [T:MIE-022]
- According to UGA, a sausage is shelf stable only when its water activity is below 0.85 and its pH is under 5.3. [T:MIE-023]
- Without an effective starter culture, fermentation temperatures of 80–100 °F can let dangerous bacteria grow in the meat. [T:MIE-025]

## Why

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for ’nduja from a checked source. [T:-]
