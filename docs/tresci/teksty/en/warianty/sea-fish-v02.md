# Barrel-salted herring (matjes) in oil: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for herring salted at home in a barrel, matjes style, and then kept in oil with onion. [T:-]

## !

- According to the FDA, nematode larvae have survived 28 days in an 80° salinometer brine (21% salt by weight). [T:MIE-050]
- The EU requires marinated, salted and other treated fish products to be frozen if the treatment is not enough to kill live parasites. [T:MIE-053]
- The FDA considers uneviscerated (ungutted) fish that are salt-cured, dried or smoked a potentially life-threatening health hazard. [T:MIE-067]
- According to the FDA, brining and pickling can reduce the parasite hazard in fish, but they do not eliminate it. [T:MIE-049]

## Why

According to the FDA, gut any fish before salting, drying, pickling or fermenting it. [T:MIE-061]

The FDA may act against uneviscerated salt-cured, dried or smoked fish more than 5 inches long. [T:MIE-069]

According to the FDA, freezing and storing fish at −20 °C (−4 °F) or below for 7 days in total kills parasites. [T:MIE-046]
