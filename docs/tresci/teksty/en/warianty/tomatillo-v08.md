# Cape gooseberry preserves in syrup: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for Cape gooseberry (Physalis peruviana) preserves in syrup: JarAtlas has no recipe for them from a checked source. [T:-]

## !

- A boiling-water bath is suitable only for acid foods, such as fruits, pickles, jams and fruit butters, and only with the research-based process times of the USDA Complete Guide to Home Canning. [T:SZY-054]
- NCHFP warns not to reduce the sugar in traditional recipes: too little sugar prevents gelling and may allow yeasts and molds to grow. [T:SLO-001]
- Unripe ground cherry (Physalis) fruits and the plant’s stems and leaves contain toxins, as in other nightshade plants; only ripe fruits are eaten. [T:SUS-301]
