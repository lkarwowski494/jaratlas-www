# Radish in soy sauce: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for daikon or black radish in soy sauce. [T:-]

## !

- Almost any food that is not very acidic, with a pH above 4.6, can support the growth of Clostridium botulinum and toxin production. [T:SLO-023]
- According to the FDA, the minimum temperature for growth and toxin production by Clostridium botulinum type E and nonproteolytic types B and F is 38°F (3.3°C). [T:MIE-059]

## Why

The FDA list gives white radishes a pH of about 5.52–5.69; it has no entry for black radish. [T:ACI-111]

Radish is a vegetable, and all fresh vegetables except most tomatoes are low-acid foods with a pH above 4.6. [T:ACI-005]

This pickle uses soy sauce, not vinegar. [T:-]

This pickle is meant to be kept in the refrigerator. [T:-]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for daikon or black radish in soy sauce from a checked source. [T:-]
