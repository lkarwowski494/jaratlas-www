# Fermented cornelian cherries in olive oil: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for cornelian cherries that are fermented and then kept in olive oil: JarAtlas has no recipe for them from a checked source. [T:-]

## !

- There are no validated processes for vegetables and herbs in oil made at home; in the USA, the rules for such products apply only to commercial processors. [T:OLE-028]
- Appearance, smell and taste cannot tell you whether vegetables in oil carry a botulism hazard. [T:OLE-027]

## Why

JarAtlas found no checked source with a home method for fruit in oil, so it applies the same caution as for vegetables in oil. [T:-]

JarAtlas also has no checked recipe for fermenting cornelian cherries before they go into the oil. [T:-]
