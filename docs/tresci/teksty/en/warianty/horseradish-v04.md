# Horseradish ćwikła (beetroot and horseradish relish): why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for horseradish ćwikła: JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for it. [T:-]

## !

- According to Oregon State University Extension, a recipe for fresh or quick pack pickles must call for vinegar of 5% acidity and have at least as much vinegar as water, unless it is an OSU or USDA tested recipe. [T:ACI-751]
- Use only vinegar labelled 5% acidity; white and cider vinegar can be used interchangeably; do not use wine vinegars, homemade vinegar or lemon juice. [T:ACI-509]
- Untested recipes can be prepared and stored in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks; do not store them on the counter. [T:ACI-508]

## Why

According to an RMF24 recipe, ćwikła uses a tablespoon of vinegar or lemon juice, and the horseradish is covered with 5–6 tablespoons of boiling water. [T:ACI-867]

That recipe has less vinegar than water and allows lemon juice instead of vinegar. [T:ACI-867,ACI-751,ACI-509]

It gives no number of days in the refrigerator. [T:ACI-867]

Beetroot and horseradish are vegetables, and all fresh vegetables except most tomatoes are low-acid foods with a pH above 4.6. [T:ACI-005]

JarAtlas has a card for the tested NCHFP horseradish sauce, which is kept in the refrigerator and is not canned. [T:ACI-035]
