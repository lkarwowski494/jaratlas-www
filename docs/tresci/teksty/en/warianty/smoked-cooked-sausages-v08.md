# Homemade frankfurters and wieners: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for homemade frankfurters and wieners made from meat chopped fine with ice (Polish parówki i serdelki domowe). [T:-]

## !

- UGA recommends cooking products made from ground pork and beef to an internal temperature of 160 °F. [T:MIE-021]
- Too much nitrate or nitrite can be toxic to people. [T:MIE-011]

## Why

UGA describes frankfurters, bologna, knockwurst and Vienna sausages as made from a finely ground, emulsified mixture, but its cooked sausage recipes include none of this kind. [T:MIE-152]

JarAtlas has found no tested home recipe for frankfurters or wieners from a checked source. [T:-]
