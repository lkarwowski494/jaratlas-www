# Stone-dried quark (kurt): why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for drying quark into kurt. [T:-]

## !

- In studies of kurut (dried yogurt or buttermilk) in Turkey, Staphylococcus aureus was found in 16% of samples from the Elazığ area and in 60% of retail samples from Van, and Escherichia coli in 12% of the Elazığ samples. [T:MLE-070]

## Why

JarAtlas has found no checked source that describes a home process for drying quark into kurt. [T:-]

Without a tested process, JarAtlas does not give drying times or temperatures for it. [T:-]
