# Cauliflower in tomato sauce: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for cauliflower in tomato sauce, canned in jars. [T:-]

## !

- Pressure canning is the only recommended method for canning meat, poultry, seafood and vegetables; using a boiling-water bath for these foods poses a real risk of botulism poisoning. [T:SZY-006]
- Penn State Extension says there are no scientifically tested recipes for canning broccoli or cauliflower as a plain vegetable. [T:SZY-024]
- Penn State Extension says that broccoli, cauliflower, cabbage, kohlrabi and Brussels sprouts are not suitable for pressure canning. [T:SZY-025]

## Why

The NCHFP recipe for mixed vegetables does not allow leafy greens, dried beans, cream-style corn, winter squash, sweet potatoes, broccoli, cauliflower or cabbage as substitutes. [T:SZY-022]

Most mixtures of low-acid and acid foods are also low-acid, unless the recipe has enough lemon juice, citric acid or vinegar to make them acid foods. [T:SZY-002]

JarAtlas does not treat a dish as an acid food because it tastes sour, or because acid was added without a tested recipe. [T:-]

JarAtlas has found no tested home recipe for canned cauliflower in tomato sauce from a checked source. [T:-]
