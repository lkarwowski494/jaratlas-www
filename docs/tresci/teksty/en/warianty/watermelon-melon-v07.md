# Candied watermelon and melon rind: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for candied watermelon rind or candied melon rind. [T:-]

## !

- According to an FDA table of approximate values, watermelon has a pH of 5.2–5.8. [T:SLO-024]
- According to the same FDA table, cantaloupe melon has a pH of 6.17–7.13. [T:SLO-025]
- According to a University of Missouri Extension newsletter, candied fruit in general retains a bit of moisture, so it is kept in an airtight container in the refrigerator to prevent mold and used within a week. [T:SLO-069]

## Why

JarAtlas has found no checked source for the pH of the rind of either fruit. [T:-]

JarAtlas has found no checked source for candied melon rind, and no tested process for rind kept in syrup. [T:-]

JarAtlas has found no checked source that says how long candied watermelon rind keeps. [T:-]
