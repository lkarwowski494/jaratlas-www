# Soured raw milk: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for soured milk made by letting raw (unpasteurized) milk turn sour on its own. [T:-]

## !

- Raw (unpasteurized) milk can carry dangerous germs such as Salmonella, E. coli, Listeria and Campylobacter. [T:MLE-001]
- The FDA says these germs are especially dangerous to people with weakened immune systems, children, older adults and pregnant women. [T:MLE-002]
- Listeria is often found in raw milk, and in pregnant women it can cause miscarriage, or illness or death of the newborn baby. [T:MLE-003]
- The FDA lists unpasteurized milk or cream, cheeses made from unpasteurized milk and yogurt made from unpasteurized milk as high-risk choices. [T:MLE-004]
- Raw milk does not kill dangerous pathogens by itself. [T:MLE-005]

## Why

Milk soured this way is not heated at any step. [T:-]

JarAtlas has found no tested home recipe for soured milk from raw milk from a checked source. [T:-]
