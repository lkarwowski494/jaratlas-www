# Red onion and cranberry jam: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for red onion and cranberry jam kept in the refrigerator. [T:-]

## !

- According to Ask Extension, onions are a low-acid vegetable, so they must be pressure canned to prevent botulism. [T:ACI-524]
- Almost any food that is not very acidic, with a pH above 4.6, can support the growth of Clostridium botulinum and toxin production. [T:SLO-023]

## Why

According to an FDA table of approximate values, red onions have a pH of 5.3–5.8. [T:SLO-033]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe that uses cranberries to acidify onion jam. [T:-]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for red onion and cranberry jam kept in the refrigerator from a checked source. [T:-]
