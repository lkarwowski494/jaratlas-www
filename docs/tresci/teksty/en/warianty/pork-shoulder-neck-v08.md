# Pork luncheon meat with curing salt in jars: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for pork luncheon meat, a dense, packed mass of ground pork with curing salt, canned in jars (Polish mielonka). [T:-]

## !

- Pressure canning is the only recommended method for canning meat, poultry, seafood and vegetables; using a boiling-water bath for these foods poses a real risk of botulism poisoning. [T:SZY-006]
- Do not attempt to can pureed vegetables, red meats or poultry at home: proper processing times for pureed foods have not been determined for home use. [T:SZY-014]
- Tyndallization (repeated heating with pauses for spores to germinate) is not considered fully reliable, because some superdormant spores do not germinate between heatings. [T:SZY-009]

## Why

Botulinum spores are very hard to destroy at boiling-water temperatures. [T:SZY-004]

JarAtlas has found no tested home recipe for canning pork luncheon meat from a checked source. [T:-]
