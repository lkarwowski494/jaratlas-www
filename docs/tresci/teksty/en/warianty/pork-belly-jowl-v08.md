# Tyndallized bacon in aspic in jars: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for roasted or simmered pork belly in aspic, heated in jars several times (tyndallization). [T:-]

## !

- Pressure canning is the only recommended method for canning meat, poultry, seafood and vegetables; using a boiling-water bath for these foods poses a real risk of botulism poisoning. [T:SZY-006]
- NCHFP recommends canning low-acid foods, and tomatoes and tomato products, only according to the procedures in the USDA Complete Guide to Home Canning. [T:SZY-007]
- Tyndallization (repeated heating with pauses for spores to germinate) is not considered fully reliable, because some superdormant spores do not germinate between heatings. [T:SZY-009]

## Why

Botulinum spores are very hard to destroy at boiling-water temperatures. [T:SZY-004]

Red meats and poultry are low-acid foods. [T:SZY-001]

JarAtlas has found no tested home recipe for canning pork belly in aspic from a checked source. [T:-]
