# Peaches in syrup with bourbon or brandy: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for peaches canned in syrup with bourbon or brandy. [T:-]

## !

- Syrup helps keep the flavor, color and shape of the fruit, but it does not prevent spoilage. [T:ACI-067]
- Do not use the NCHFP peach canning process for white-flesh peaches: some varieties are higher in pH, and there is no tested process or acidification for them. [T:E3-001]

## Why

NCHFP has a tested recipe for canning halved or sliced peaches in a boiling-water canner, for yellow-flesh peaches. [T:SLO-098,SLO-128]

In that recipe the peaches are packed in very light, light or medium syrup, or in water, apple juice or white grape juice. [T:SLO-129]

Bourbon and brandy are additions outside this tested recipe. [T:-]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for peaches canned in syrup with bourbon or brandy from a checked source. [T:-]

JarAtlas has a card for yellow-flesh peaches canned by the tested NCHFP recipe, without alcohol. [T:-]
