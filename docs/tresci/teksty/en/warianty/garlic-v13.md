# Pasteurized garlic cream: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for garlic cream, a smooth garlic purée, pasteurized in jars. [T:-]

## !

- Pressure canning is the only recommended method for canning meat, poultry, seafood and vegetables; using a boiling-water bath for these foods poses a real risk of botulism poisoning. [T:SZY-006]
- NCHFP says not to try canning puréed vegetables, red meats or poultry at home, because proper processing times for puréed foods have not been determined for home use. [T:SZY-014]

## Why

All fresh vegetables except most tomatoes are low-acid foods. [T:SZY-001]
