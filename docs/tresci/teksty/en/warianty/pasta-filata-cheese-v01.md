# Fresh mozzarella stretched in hot water (NMSU recipe)

This card follows the NMSU Extension “pizza cheese” (mozzarella) recipe: curd made with yogurt as the culture is stretched in hot water at 180 °F (82 °C), brined for 24 hours and kept in the refrigerator for up to a week. [T:MLE-056]

## Safety first

Make this cheese only from pasteurized milk; raw milk must be pasteurized before it is used for cheese. [T:MLE-015]

If you keep the fresh cheese in water, eat it within 2 days. [T:MLE-086]

## What this card covers

The NMSU recipe does not measure pH, so this card gives no pH value. [T:-]

## Ingredients

NMSU uses 1 gallon (3.79 l) of 2% milk, or 2 quarts (1.9 l) of whole milk plus 2 quarts of skim milk. [T:MLE-083]

The culture is 1/4 cup (57 g) of fresh plain yogurt. [T:MLE-083]

The rennet is 1 tablet or 1 teaspoon of liquid rennet, dissolved in 1/2 cup of water. [T:MLE-083]

For the brine, NMSU uses 2 lb (907 g) of salt per gallon (3.78 l) of water. [T:MLE-083]

## Making the curd

Heat the milk to 90 °F (32.2 °C), add the yogurt and stir slowly for 15 minutes, keeping that temperature. [T:MLE-084]

Add the dissolved rennet and stir for 3–5 minutes. [T:MLE-084]

Cover and keep at 90 °F (32.2 °C) for about 30 minutes, until the milk has coagulated. [T:MLE-084]

Cut the curd into 1/2-inch (1.27 cm) cubes and leave them for 15 minutes, stirring now and then. [T:MLE-084]

Raise the temperature slowly to 118 °F (47.7 °C) over 45 minutes and hold it for 15 minutes more. [T:MLE-084]

Let the curd settle, remove the whey and move the mat of curd to a flat pan that you can keep warm. [T:MLE-084]

Do not cut the mat; turn it over every 15 minutes for 2 hours. [T:MLE-084]

## Stretching and brining

Cut the mat into strips 1–2 inches (2.5–5 cm) wide and cover them with hot water at 180 °F (82.2 °C). [T:MLE-085]

With wooden spoons, tumble and stretch the curd under the hot water for about 15 minutes, until it is elastic. [T:MLE-085]

Shape it by hand into a ball or loaf and put it into cold water at 40 °F (4.4 °C) for about 1 hour. [T:MLE-085]

Then put it into saturated brine kept at 40–50 °F (4.4–10 °C), in a heavy plastic, glass or pottery container. [T:MLE-085]

Cover any exposed parts of the cheese with salt or brine and leave it in the brine for 24 hours. [T:MLE-085]

Take it out, let it dry for several hours, wrap it in plastic wrap or a sealable bag and refrigerate it. [T:MLE-085]

## Storing

Brined and wrapped, the cheese keeps in the refrigerator for up to a week. [T:MLE-056]

Cheese kept in water must be eaten within 2 days. [T:MLE-086]

## Who should be careful

Pregnant women should avoid cheeses made from unpasteurized milk. [T:MLE-049]
