# Cold-smoked salmon: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for salmon smoked at home with cold smoke. [T:-]

## !

- According to the FDA, C. botulinum type E, and nonproteolytic types B and F, can grow and form toxin at temperatures as low as 38 °F (3.3 °C). [T:MIE-059]
- According to a University of Alaska guide to smoking fish, cold-smoke temperatures are not hot enough to kill bacteria and may even help them grow. [T:MIE-076]
- …so the product can spoil or become unsafe to eat before it is fully smoked. [T:MIE-077]
- Even vacuum-packed smoked fish must be kept in the refrigerator or freezer, never at room temperature. [T:MIE-079]

## Why

According to the FDA, C. botulinum type E is the most common form in fresh water and the sea, and it should be assumed to be present in any raw fish, especially in the guts. [T:MIE-060]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for cold-smoked salmon from a checked source. [T:-]
