# Kohlrabi canned in brine: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for kohlrabi canned in a salt brine. [T:-]

## !

- Penn State Extension says that broccoli, cauliflower, cabbage, kohlrabi and Brussels sprouts are not suitable for pressure canning. [T:SZY-025]

## Why

All fresh vegetables except most tomatoes are low-acid foods. [T:SZY-001]

JarAtlas has found no tested home recipe for kohlrabi canned in brine from a checked source. [T:-]

JarAtlas has a card for frozen kohlrabi. [T:-]
