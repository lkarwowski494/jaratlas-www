# Milk caps under clarified butter: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for saffron milk caps (rydze) fried in clarified butter and sealed in jars under a layer of fat. [T:-]

## !

- Drying and cooking do not destroy the toxins of poisonous mushrooms, and only an expert can tell edible species from poisonous ones. [T:SUS-035]

## Why

NCHFP says: do not can wild mushrooms. [T:OLE-040]

Botulism has been linked to mushrooms covered in oil: in the USA in 1998, one case to home-prepared mushrooms in oil, and in Britain two cases to mushrooms in oil. [T:OLE-026]

JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for mushrooms sealed in jars under fat from a checked source. [T:-]

## What you can do instead

NCHFP’s page on freezing mushrooms, which does not mention wild mushrooms, says that mushrooms can be frozen after they are steamed or heated in fat in a fry pan. [T:OLE-041]
