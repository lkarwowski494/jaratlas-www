# Chili oil (la-yu) for the refrigerator, eaten by the next day

This card is for chili oil (la-yu), chili in oil kept in the refrigerator and eaten soon. [T:-] This card follows a general rule for vegetables in oil from food-safety authorities, not a recipe tested for this product. [T:-] It gives no amounts or cooking times; it gives the rules for preparing and storing it. [T:-] The German Federal Institute for Risk Assessment (BfR) says homemade vegetables in oil are kept in the refrigerator and eaten no later than the day after they are made. [T:OLE-077]

## !

- Keep the refrigerator at 40 °F (4 °C) or lower. [T:OLE-057]
- Eat it no later than the day after you make it, and throw away what is left. [T:OLE-077]
- Never keep it at room temperature: vegetables in oil, including partly dried ones, give C. botulinum good conditions to grow (no oxygen, the pH and the water in the vegetables), and at room temperature the bacterium can multiply quickly. [T:OLE-072]
- Do not make it in advance to store: the BfR says that with vegetables in oil a household cannot make sure that C. botulinum will not grow and form toxin, and it advises against making such products in advance for storage. [T:OLE-076]

## Why this needs care

The BfR names chili among the vegetables in oil with which a household cannot make sure that C. botulinum will not grow and form toxin. [T:OLE-076]

This chili oil is not a condiment to keep in the cupboard or to make in advance: the BfR advises against making vegetables in oil and herb oils in advance for storage. [T:OLE-076,OLE-087]

This applies to partly dried chili too: vegetables in oil, including partly dried ones, give C. botulinum good conditions to grow. [T:OLE-072]

If you add garlic, the same rules apply, because the BfR names garlic too. [T:OLE-076]

Appearance, smell and taste cannot tell you whether vegetables in oil carry a botulism hazard. [T:OLE-027]

## Preparing

Make only as much as you will eat by the day after you make it. [T:OLE-077]

Start with a clean glass container: wash it thoroughly and boil it for 10 minutes. [T:OLE-058]

Choose good-quality chili and other ingredients. [T:OLE-058]

## Storing

Keep the chili oil in the refrigerator at 40 °F (4 °C) or lower. [T:OLE-057]

Eat it no later than the day after you made it, and throw away what is left. [T:OLE-077]
