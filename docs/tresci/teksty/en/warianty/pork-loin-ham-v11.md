# Dry-cured ham eaten raw: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for a dry-cured ham of the prosciutto or jamón kind, salted and dried in several stages and eaten without cooking (Polish szynka dojrzewająca). [T:-]

## !

- According to University of Kentucky Extension, a country ham is cleaned of excess cure and mold before cooking, and the publication gives ways to cook it (frying slices, simmering, baking to 140 °F inside), not eating it raw. [T:MIE-154]
- An Oregon State University Extension guide gives no procedure for cured meats eaten without cooking, such as prosciutto and salami, and says to cook bacon before eating. [T:MIE-155]
- Too much nitrate or nitrite can be toxic to people. [T:MIE-011]

## Why

A Virginia Tech recipe for dry-cured ham gives 2 ounces of saltpeter or sodium nitrite per 100 pounds of meat; for sodium nitrite, that is twice the US limit for dry curing. [T:MIE-013,MIE-002]

JarAtlas has found no tested home recipe for a dry-cured ham eaten raw from a checked source. [T:-]
