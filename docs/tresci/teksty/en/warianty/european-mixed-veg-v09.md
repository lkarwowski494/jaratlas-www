# Hungarian lecsó in jars: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for Hungarian lecsó, a vegetable stew of peppers, tomatoes and onions, canned in jars. [T:-]

## !

- Pressure canning is the only recommended method for canning meat, poultry, seafood and vegetables; using a boiling-water bath for these foods poses a real risk of botulism poisoning. [T:SZY-006]
- Canning zucchini without pickling is no longer recommended, because research-based processing times are uncertain. [T:SZY-026]
- In the NCHFP spaghetti sauce without meat, do not increase the proportion of onions, peppers or mushrooms. [T:SZY-028]

## Why

Most mixtures of low-acid and acid foods are also low-acid, unless their recipes include enough lemon juice, citric acid or vinegar to make them acid foods. [T:SZY-002]

JarAtlas does not treat a dish as an acid food because it tastes sour, or because acid was added without a tested recipe. [T:-]

JarAtlas has found no tested home recipe for canning lecsó from a checked source. [T:-]

The warning about zucchini applies when the stew includes zucchini or other summer squash; the other warnings apply in every case. [T:-]
