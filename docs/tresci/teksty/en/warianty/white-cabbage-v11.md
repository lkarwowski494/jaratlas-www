# Korean-style cabbage with chili: why there is no recipe here

There is no recipe here for Korean-style cabbage with chili: JarAtlas has found no tested recipe for it. [T:-]

## !

- According to Oregon State University Extension, a recipe for fresh or quick pack pickles must call for vinegar of 5% acidity and have at least as much vinegar as water, unless it is an OSU or USDA tested recipe. [T:ACI-751]
- Use only vinegar labelled 5% acidity; white and cider vinegar can be used interchangeably; do not use wine vinegars, homemade vinegar or lemon juice. [T:ACI-509]
- Untested recipes can be prepared and stored in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks; do not store them on the counter. [T:ACI-508]

## Why

A recipe from the hawaii.edu recipe archive (Chili Pickled Cabbage – Kim Chee) uses napa cabbage with equal amounts of white vinegar and light soy sauce and does not give the acidity of the vinegar. [T:ACI-868]

According to that recipe, the sealed jar is left in a cool place for at least 24 hours before using, and it keeps for up to a week in the refrigerator. [T:ACI-868]

Refrigerator pickles are not heat processed, so they must be kept in the refrigerator. [T:ACI-501]

Cabbage is a vegetable, and all fresh vegetables except most tomatoes are low-acid foods with a pH above 4.6. [T:ACI-005]
